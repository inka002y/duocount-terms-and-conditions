Support

Last updated · 2026-10-26

DuoCount is developed by Seima Shibutani. For support or privacy requests, email inka008y@gmail.com. Do not include sensitive information or a complete backup unless it is necessary and you choose to send it.

The count is not increasing

Check Settings for tracking, sensor and display status. Counting needs supported hardware, closed-to-open evidence and the active app. App suspension or missing display evidence prevents counting. A web or Expo Go preview does not verify real device detection; demo results are samples.

Export or remove records

Settings provides CSV/JSON export and recorded-history deletion. Turn off demo mode to manage real records. Exported files and OS backups must be managed separately. There is no account-deletion procedure because DuoCount has no account registration.

Advertising or a problem report

Where required, change consent in Settings → Advertising privacy choices. Ads may be unavailable offline or after refusal, while the counter remains usable. For a bug report, describe the steps and include the app/OS version and device model; redact private details from screenshots.
