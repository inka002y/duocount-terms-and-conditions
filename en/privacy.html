Privacy Policy

Last updated · 2026-10-26

This policy explains how Seima Shibutani (the developer) handles information in DuoCount, an app for recording observed device openings. Contact: inka008y@gmail.com.

1. Records kept on your device

DuoCount stores daily counts, recent event times and preferences locally to show your history and statistics. Hinge and active-display signals are processed for counting while the app is active; raw signal streams are not stored as a history or sent to the developer. Demo records are separate. There is no DuoCount account, record-upload server or developer-run analytics service.

2. Retention, export and deletion

Daily totals remain until you clear or replace them. The detailed event list keeps up to 200 entries; older details are removed without removing daily totals. Settings → Clear recorded history removes recorded history, not preferences. Demo reset does not delete real records. CSV/JSON exports and OS backups can contain app data; you control their storage and deletion. Import replaces history after confirmation. Removing the app removes its local container, but may leave exports or backups. The developer cannot retrieve or remotely delete records stored only on your device.

3. Google AdMob and consent

The iOS app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and User Messaging Platform (UMP) for banners, advertising measurement, fraud prevention and privacy choices. Google may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from IP), device and app information, permitted device identifiers, crash and performance data, ad impressions and interactions, and other product interactions. DuoCount does not include fold counts, event history or backups in ad requests. It requests non-personalized ads; these can still process data and use device storage. The web preview makes no ad requests. See Google's explanations for its processing and safeguards.

Google may use cookies, local storage or mobile identifiers to limit repeated ads and create aggregate ad reports, even for non-personalized ads. Consent is requested where legally required, including applicable EEA, UK and Swiss requirements.

  • How Google uses information from apps

4. Your advertising choices

At each app launch, UMP checks applicable privacy requirements before ads initialize. If UMP does not permit ad requests or the check fails, ads remain off. Where required, Settings → Advertising privacy choices lets you review or withdraw choices. Apple's tracking permission, if requested, is separate and can be changed in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Refusal does not disable the counter. Reading this policy or using the app is not a substitute for required consent. Google also provides account-level advertising controls.

  • Google advertising controls

5. Contact, purposes and retention

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and attachments to answer your request and investigate problems. Include only what is needed; do not send backups unless you choose to share them. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations, then deleted. Where applicable, processing relies on consent for advertising that requires it, responding to your request, legitimate interests in secure support, or legal obligations; Google's own notice explains its processing bases.

6. Providers and international processing

Google, email providers and the hosting provider may process information in other countries under their applicable terms, safeguards and privacy notices. Google controls retention of SDK data; clearing DuoCount history does not erase data already processed by Google. Use Google's privacy tools or contact us for assistance. The developer does not sell fold records or send them to advertising providers. Disclosures of support information may be made where required by law.

Where a consent message identifies additional advertising partners, those named partners may process the information for the purposes and choices shown there. Review the partner names and privacy links in that message or in Advertising privacy choices when available. A website you choose to open from an ad follows its own privacy policy.

The developer requires third parties receiving information through the app to provide safeguards at least equivalent to the protection described in this policy and required by applicable App Store privacy rules.

7. Website and device security

The public legal pages are prepared for GitHub Pages hosting. These static pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, third-party fonts or cookies added by the developer. When accessed on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process IP and request information under its privacy statement. Local in-app copies work offline. Device records use the app's local storage and OS protections, not a separately encrypted vault; protect your device and exports.

  • GitHub Privacy Statement

8. Children and privacy rights

DuoCount is a general utility and is not directed to children under 13 or any higher applicable local age. Contact us if a child has sent personal information. Depending on your location, you may have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and withdrawal of consent, and a right to complain to your data-protection authority. Contact inka008y@gmail.com to exercise rights concerning information held by the developer. Device-only records can be exported or deleted in Settings; Google handles requests concerning its own processing.

9. Policy changes

The date above identifies this policy version. Material changes will be explained in the app or on this page, and fresh consent will be requested where required. A policy update does not automatically grant consent to new processing.
